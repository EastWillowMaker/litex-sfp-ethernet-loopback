Stimulus and measurement
------------------------
Both ingress directions run concurrently through two real LiteEth MACs at
125 MHz physical RX/TX clocks with independent phase offsets. Each side sends
48 wire frames: 36 valid 1514-byte IPv4 bodies, six IPv6 frames, and six
1514-byte IPv4 frames with one FCS byte corrupted. The order is six valid
frames, IPv6, bad-FCS, repeated six times. Every frame has a 12-PHY-clock
interframe gap. A valid frame follows every rejected pair.

The bounded measurement starts immediately before ingress and ends after the
input bursts plus 8192 system-clock drain cycles. The reported rate is
per-direction captured Ethernet payload bits divided by this measured
simulation window; preamble and FCS bytes are excluded. The pass test compares
every complete output physical frame against the expected preamble, body and
independently generated zlib FCS before checking the 500 Mbps threshold.

Results
-------

Configuration                  Frames out/dir  Mbps/dir  Window(ns)  Outcome
Baseline single-frame, 50 MHz       8 / 36        141       684540    FAIL
Baseline single-frame, 125 MHz     18 / 36        371       586240    FAIL
Queued bridge, 50 MHz              15 / 36        265       684540    FAIL
Queued bridge, 125 MHz             36 / 36        743       586240    PASS

Per-direction bridge counters were identical in both paths:

Baseline single-frame, 50 MHz:
  rx=8 tx=8 rejected=15 error=2 overflow=7 ipv6_drop=6; MAC CRC errors=2
Baseline single-frame, 125 MHz:
  rx=18 tx=18 rejected=30 error=6 overflow=18 ipv6_drop=6; MAC CRC errors=6
Queued bridge, 50 MHz:
  rx=15 tx=15 rejected=8 error=2 overflow=0 ipv6_drop=6; MAC CRC errors=2
Queued bridge, 125 MHz:
  rx=36 tx=36 rejected=12 error=6 overflow=0 ipv6_drop=6; MAC CRC errors=6

At queued 50 MHz, the bridge counters account for 23 of the 48 physically
driven frames per ingress direction; 25 do not reach the bridge stream
counters. The harness exposes MAC CRC status, but no dedicated MAC CDC/drop
counter, so the upstream loss is inferred and is not directly attributed by a
hardware counter. At queued 125 MHz, all 48 frames per input direction are
accounted for, and all 36 valid frames per output direction match exactly.
